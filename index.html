Guin's Garden

a gentle bug catching adventure

How to play

  • Walk by holding your thumb anywhere on the left side of the screen and sliding it.
  • Swing your net with the big yellow button.
  • Bugs get nervous when you run at them. When you see a !, slow down and creep closer.
  • Different bugs like different places and different times of day. Come back at night for fireflies and moths!
  • Bees can sting. Swing at a bee and miss, or poke the beehive, and she will chase you with a little red cross over her head. Run away, or catch her quickly! A sting leaves you seeing stars for a moment.
  • Go home (the little cottage) to read about every bug you have caught and to build terrariums.
  • Catching bugs earns ✦ sparkles. Spend them on decorations in the box by your bed.

On a computer you can use the arrow keys (or WASD) to walk, hold shift to tiptoe, and press space to swing.
