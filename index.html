How to play

👆
WalkTap where you want to go, or slide your thumb to steer.
🦋
Catch bugsCreep up close and tap NET. If a bug shows a !, slow down.
🎣
Go fishingStand by the water and tap FISH. When the bobber dips, tap CATCH.
🍎
Pick thingsWalk up to fruit, vegetables, berries or flowers and tap PICK.
🐶
Make friendsWalk up to an animal, tap the green button, then keep very still.
🐝
Mind the beesLeave bees and their hive alone, or they sting.
🏠
Go homeRead your Critter Compendium and build tanks for your bugs, fish and friends.
✦

What’s new

Let a friend play

A visitor gets a garden of their very own to explore. Nothing they do changes Guin’s garden — her bugs, fish, tanks and sparkles are kept safe and separate, and they come straight back when the visit ends.

A visit is not saved forever. When you finish the visit the guest garden is cleared out, ready for the next friend.
