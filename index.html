How to play

  • Walk by holding your thumb anywhere on the left side of the screen and sliding it.
  • Swing your net with the big yellow button.
  • Bugs get nervous when you run at them. When you see a !, slow down and creep closer.
  • Different bugs like different places and different times of day. Come back at night for fireflies and moths, and after dark on the beach for ghost crabs.
  • Go fishing anywhere there is water. Stand on the bank facing it and a blue FISH button appears. Cast, watch the shadow swim over, and when the bobber goes under and you see a !, tap CATCH. Bigger shadows are bigger fish.
  • Every water holds different fish: the Lily Pond, Pebble Stream up in the hills, the Winding River, Gull Inlet where the river meets the sea, and the open sea off Shell Beach. Follow the stream downhill and see where it takes you.
  • You can paddle straight across the little stream, but the river and the sea are too deep, so look for a way round.
  • Out at The Tidepools, past Shell Beach, you can wade into the rock pools and net crabs, sea stars, urchins and sea slugs with your net. (In a real rock pool we look and put everything back exactly where we found it.)
  • Making friends. Some animals are not for catching at all — hummingbirds, frogs, bats, dogs, cats and parrots. Walk up to one and a green button appears telling you the right way to say hello to that kind of animal: put out a feeder, keep very still, ask its owner first. Tap it, then stand completely still while the little heart fills up. Wander about and it slips back a bit, but it never empties and the animal never runs off for good.
  • Every friend you make gets a page in the Friends Book with a Good manners note — the real way to be kind and safe with that animal. Tap Ask them along on any friend’s page and they will follow you round the garden all day.
  • Build a Garden Habitat from the New Tank button: a corner of the garden out under the sky, with a feeder, a bat house, a kennel, a cat basket and a parrot perch to buy. Your friends come and visit it — they are never shut in, and you can send them off again whenever you like.
  • Bees can sting. Swing at a bee and miss, or poke the beehive, and she will chase you with a little red cross over her head. Run away, or catch her quickly! A sting leaves you seeing stars for a moment.
  • Go home (the little cottage) to read about everything you have caught and to build tanks. There are four kinds: a terrarium for bugs, a fish tank for fish, a hybrid tank with a grassy bank above the water that holds both, and a garden habitat outdoors for your animal friends.
  • The rock-pool creatures — crabs, sea stars, urchins, limpets, periwinkles and sea slugs — need water to live in, so they go in a fish tank or a hybrid tank, never a dry terrarium. They potter about on the sand at the bottom.
  • Catching bugs earns ✦ sparkles. Spend them in the decoration box by your bed — there are things for every kind of tank, from ferns and fairy houses to coral, sea grass and a treasure chest.
  • Settings (on the title screen, or in the Garden Menu) turns the music, the garden sounds and the beeps up and down on their own.
  • A friend can have a turn with Let a friend play on the title screen. They get a garden of their own to explore, and yours is kept completely safe until they finish.

On a computer you can use the arrow keys (or WASD) to walk, hold shift to tiptoe, and press space to swing. F casts a fishing line and E says hello to an animal.
